About Radiance Studio

Keeping the Radiance of Your Hair

Our Story

Radiance Studio was founded in 2015 in Johannesburg by a small group of passionate hairstylists who shared one simple belief: that great hair care should make people feel confident, cared for, and truly seen. What started as a single-chair salon has grown into a full-service hair studio, trusted by clients across the city for everything from everyday braids to special-occasion styling. Over the years we've stayed true to our roots — personal attention, honest advice, and a genuine love for the craft of hairdressing. We believe that every client deserves to leave our studio feeling radiant, and we work tirelessly to make that happen.

Our Mission

To provide every client with expert hair care in a welcoming environment, using quality products and techniques that protect the health of their hair while helping them look and feel radiant. We are committed to continuous learning and innovation, ensuring that our team stays at the forefront of hair trends and techniques.

Our Vision

To be Johannesburg's most trusted hair studio, recognised for our skill, creativity, and the genuine relationships we build with every client who walks through our doors. We aim to create a space where clients feel valued and inspired, and where our team can grow and thrive as professionals in the hair industry.

Meet Our Team

Karabo Nkwanyana – Senior Stylist & Salon Owner

With over 12 years behind the chair, Karabo founded Radiance Studio and leads the team with a focus on precision cutting and client care. She is passionate about mentoring young stylists and fostering a collaborative studio environment.

Faith Phandlela – Colour Specialist

Faith trained in advanced colour theory and specialises in balayage, highlights, and is one of the best braiders in the business. She is known for her ability to create custom colour blends that enhance each client's natural beauty. See her colouring services.

Ariana Lyall – Hair Treatment Specialist

Ariana focuses on African hair health, offering deep-conditioning and repair treatments for damaged or chemically treated hair. She is passionate about helping clients maintain strong, healthy African hair. Ariana is also skilled in natural hair care techniques and provides guidance on maintaining hair health at home.
